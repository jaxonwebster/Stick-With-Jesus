Meet   The   Makers



Where We Started

Hi! We are Hailey and Jaxon. We fell in love in 2022 and got to thinking. Wouldn’t it be great to start something together? We put our heads together and started a sticker business at the very start of our marriage. We started oh so small.

We had an iPad, a Cricut, and a dream. We didn’t even know how to waterproof our stickers yet (rookie mistake!) Neither of us had a background in art, but we taught ourselves. Over time, we started making things we were really proud of. (And yes, our stickers are 100% waterproof now!)

One day, we received an order from someone we didn’t know—not a friend, not a family member. Her name is Sandra from North Carolina. She was our very first hope that maybe we really could make a business happen.

The rest has been a blur. We did our business throughout college, working other jobs and keeping our grades up. We both graduated (huzzah!), we got pregnant, and we had a sweet baby boy! And guess what’s been there all along? Our sticker business. We love sharing art with the world. Especially art that inspires people to remember Jesus in all that we do. So we hope you stick around with us! But most of all, we hope you stick with Jesus. We love you! Thank you for being part of our wonderful story!
